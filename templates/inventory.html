Inventory Valuation at Catalog Selling Price

The stock records below represent the application's live inventory across 50 stores. Stock Value is calculated as stock_on_hand × catalog selling_price because the historical source inventory snapshot provides physical unit counts without cost-basis valuation. Stock statuses are dynamically evaluated: OUT OF STOCK (0 units), LOW STOCK (> 0 and < reorder threshold), and NORMAL (≥ reorder threshold).
