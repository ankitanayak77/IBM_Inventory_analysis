Relative Percentile-Based Classification Methodology

Product movement classification (Fast Moving, Normal, Slow Moving) is calculated relative to the actual empirical sales velocity distribution of all catalog products during the selected time window. Sales Velocity is calculated as Total Units Sold / Number of Days in Period. Products are classified via percentile thresholds: FAST MOVING (Top 25% of products, ≥ {{ summary.fast_threshold_velocity }} units/day), SLOW MOVING (Bottom 25% of products, ≤ {{ summary.slow_threshold_velocity }} units/day), and NORMAL (Middle 50%). This is relative to the selected dataset and is not a rigid universal business standard. Historical sales baseline covers 2017-01-01 through 2018-09-30.
