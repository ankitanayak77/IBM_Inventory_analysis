Transparent Rule-Based Decision Support System (Not AI / Machine Learning)

The recommendations below are deterministic decision-support signals generated by transparent, explainable business rules. They evaluate the combination of Sales Velocity (historical units sold per day), Current Application Inventory (stock on hand across 50 stores), and Configured Reorder Thresholds. Stock Coverage is calculated as Current Stock ÷ Sales Velocity (days). Priority levels: HIGH (Fast-moving items that are out of stock or below threshold), MEDIUM (Fast-moving with < {{ summary.low_coverage_threshold_days }}d coverage, slow-moving with ample stock, or non-fast out of stock), LOW (Normal movement with healthy stock), and NONE (Signals aligned within acceptable tolerances).
