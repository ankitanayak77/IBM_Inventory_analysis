Analytical Data Semantics & Model Distinction

Historical Baseline: Sales reports cover 638 active trading days (2017-01-01 to 2018-09-30). Revenue figures represent Calculated Revenue computed at catalog retail selling price (SUM(quantity × selling_price)).
Current Application Inventory: Stock metrics and inventory snapshots describe the live SQLite inventory state across 50 branches. They are not projected backwards as historical daily balances. Valuation is calculated at catalog selling price.
